Banana Pancake Recipe

This is a recipe for traditional Cape Verdean banana pancakes. We have this breakfast or desert, and my family likes them extra sweet! This recipe is vegan friendly.

Ingredients

  • 2 ripe bananas (preferably already brown)
  • 200 grams of flour
  • 300 militiers of soy milk (or another plantbased alternative)
  • Two to three tbsp of sugar (add more or less depending on personal preference)
  • One tsp of baking powder
  • A pinch of salt
  • Sunflower oil

Instructions

  1. Start by peeling the bananas and setting them aside in a bowl.
  2. Add in the sugar and salt, and start mashing the bananas with a fork.
  3. Once the bananas are mashed together with the sugar and salt, add in the flour and soy milk. Mix these in with a fork as well. Heat up the pan to medium high with some sunflower oil while mixing.
  4. Once the batter looks smooth, add in the baking powder. Don't overmix it.
  5. Add the batter to the pan, flip the pancakes when bubbles start forming on top.
  6. Enjoy these delicious pancakes with some powdered sugar or maple syrup and cinnamon on top.
